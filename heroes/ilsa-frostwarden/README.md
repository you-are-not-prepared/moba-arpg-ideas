# Ilsa, Frostwarden

**Status:** proposal (2026-10-24). Names, numbers and talents are placeholders to discuss.

## Passive: Chilling Touch
Her spell hits slow enemies by **20% for 1.5s**. Slowed enemies take **10% more spell damage** (from anyone).

## Ultimate (R): Glacial Prison
Marks a circle (radius 3.5m, cast range 9m). After **0.75s** every enemy inside is **frozen (stunned) for 1.5s**. The circle then stays as a **slowing field (30%) for 3s**.

| Rank | Freeze | Cooldown | Mana |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.5s | 90s | 100 |
| 2 | 1.75s | 80s | 100 |
| 3 | 2s | 70s | 100 |

## Playstyle
Controls fights from range: slows whoever she hits, then locks a group down for her team. Weak when caught alone.

## Ascension (levels 6 / 11 / 18)
- **A, Deep Freeze** (passive): slows grow stronger and longer.
  - A1 Biting Cold: slow 30%. A2 Lingering Frost: slows last 1s longer.
  - A3 Shatter: +20% spell damage to slowed targets. A4 Permafrost: a target slowed 3 times in 4s is frozen for 0.75s.
- **B, Winter's Grasp** (ultimate): a bigger, longer prison.
  - B1 Wider Prison: radius +1m. B2 Swift Ice: 0.25s shorter delay.
  - B3 Endless Winter: field lasts 3s longer. B4 Cold Snap: ultimate cooldown -25%.
- **C, Glacier Walker** (hybrid): kiting.
  - C1 Ice Skates: +15% move speed while any nearby enemy is slowed. C2 Frost Armour: +150 max health.
  - C3 Hailstorm: +1 projectile on projectile spells. C4 Arctic Mind: cooldowns -10%.

## What it needs in the game
- **Slow** as a status (like stun and burn): affects move speed, shown on the health bar, synced on the game server.
- **A delayed area** that hits after a short warning and **leaves a field** on the ground.
- Talents: "while an enemy near you is slowed", "slowed 3 times → frozen".
