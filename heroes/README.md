# Heroes

Hero concepts: passive, ultimate, ascension paths, playstyle and visual identity. One folder per hero idea.

The hero proposal template from Discord is a good starting point for a hero's README.

## Proposals (all five implemented in moba-test, 2026-10-24)

| Hero | Role | Damage type | Passive | Ultimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Ilsa, Frostwarden](ilsa-frostwarden/) | Ranged mage: control | Frost | Spell hits slow; slowed enemies take more spell damage | Glacial Prison: delayed freeze, then a slowing field |
| [Corvin, Stormcaller](corvin-stormcaller/) | Ranged caster: sustained damage | Lightning | Every 4th spell chains to another enemy | Thunderhead: a storm cloud strikes nearby enemies |
| [Maela, Grovekeeper](maela-grovekeeper/) | Support: healing | Nature | Spell damage heals the nearest ally | Sanctuary: a grove that heals and speeds up allies |
| [Oren, Ember Duelist](oren-ember-duelist/) | Melee bruiser | Fire | Attacks build Heat; at 5 stacks, a burning burst | Blazing Leap: leap in, then burning attacks |
| [Thessa, Windrunner](thessa-windrunner/) | Ranged skirmisher | Air | Casting gives move speed | Eye of the Storm: no mana costs, shorter cooldowns |

Each new hero covers one damage type from the [spell affinity](../game-mechanics/spell-affinity/) hexagon; the current five (Kael, Vesh, Brann, Torvald, Nyx) are mostly Physical.

**Mechanics they'd add**, which also serve as building blocks for new spells and mods: slows (Ilsa, Maela), healing allies and areas on the ground (Maela, Ilsa), chain hits and an effect that follows the hero (Corvin), a leap/dash (Oren), and temporary cooldown/mana changes (Thessa).

**Built in this order:** Thessa, Oren, Ilsa, Maela, Corvin. Each page lists the talents as built and what changed from the proposal.
